================================================================
STORY FLOW - THE ECHO (REWORK)
Menu -> Audio Calibration -> Hub
================================================================

Muc tieu tai lieu:
- Lam lai phan story theo huong ngan gon, de nghe, de theo.
- Tap trung vao flow mo dau: Menu -> Audio Calibration -> Hub.
- Toan bo thoai la cua narrator. Player KHONG co thoai.

================================================================
FLOW TONG
================================================================

[0] MAIN MENU
- New Game
- Continue (neu co)
- Settings
- Quit

Khi chon New Game:
-> Load Audio Calibration Scene.

[1] AUDIO CALIBRATION
- Narrator huong dan kiem tra tai nghe trai/phai.
- Player xac nhan nghe dung kenh.
- Neu sai: huong dan cam lai tai nghe va thu lai.
- Luu ket qua calibration.

Khi calibration thanh cong:
-> Chuyen vao Hub.

[2] HUB INTRO
- Khoi dong chuoi node N1 -> N2 -> N3.
- Player di chuyen va nghe dan duong bang am thanh + narrator.

================================================================
HUB SCRIPT DRAFT
================================================================

# N1 - Thuc tinh

Am thanh:
- gio nhe
- khong gian trong

Sau 1-2 giay.

Narrator:
"Ban da tinh roi a?"

Dung mot chut.

"Ban co nghe thay toi khong?"

Dung.

"Neu co, hay thu buoc ve phia truoc."

Ghi chu:
- Ngan gon.
- Ro rang.
- Nguoi choi biet phai lam gi ngay.

---

Khi player toi N2.

---

# N2 - Gioi thieu Hub

Narrator:
"Tot lam."

"Ban dang o mot noi dac biet."

Dung.

"Noi day luu giu nhung am thanh tu nhieu noi khac nhau."

Dung.

"Moi am thanh deu dan den mot trai nghiem rieng."

Dung.

"Hay tien them mot chut nua."

Uu diem mong muon:
- Khong nhoi lore.
- Khong dung tu kho hieu.
- Nguoi choi van tiep tuc di chuyen.

---

Khi toi N3.

---

# N3 - Gioi thieu 3 tieng vong

Luc nay:
- tieng rung
- tieng bien
- tieng thanh pho

Phat dong thoi o ba huong khac nhau.

Narrator:
"Truoc mat ban la ba tieng vong."

Dung.

"Moi noi mang theo nhung am thanh va cau chuyen khac nhau."

Dung.

"Hay lang nghe."

Dung khoang 2 giay de nguoi choi thuc su nghe.

Sau do:
"Va chon noi ban muon cam nhan nhat."

Ket thuc doan gioi thieu.
Khong can giai thich them.

================================================================
LUU Y IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================

- Player khong co thoai trong doan mo dau.
- Tat ca huong dan va truyen tai thong tin thong qua narrator.
- Uu tien nhac doan ngan, ro, de tiep nhan bang thinh giac.
- Co the gan event trigger theo node:
  + Node N1: play HUB_N1 lines
  + Node N2: play HUB_N2 lines
  + Node N3: play HUB_N3 lines + mo lua chon khu vuc
